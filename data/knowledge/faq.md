## How does Prakhar approach class imbalance in machine learning?
Prakhar recognizes that accuracy alone is deceptive in rare-event datasets (such as credit card fraud at 0.17% or repeat purchases at 3.0%). He uses:
- **SMOTE** (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) for balanced training distributions.
- **`scale_pos_weight`** in XGBoost for penalizing minority class misclassifications.
- **PR-AUC and Precision-Recall Curves** instead of relying solely on ROC-AUC.
- **Probability Threshold Sweeping** to choose decision thresholds that maximize business objectives (e.g. capturing 81.7% of repeat revenue with only 117 targeted customers).
